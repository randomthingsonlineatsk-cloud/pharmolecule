Regulatory Skills Passport

Every certificate, checked, on one page

Six training apps give a certificate when their course is complete. Bring them together here: the page recomputes each certificate’s verification code from the scores behind it, so a changed name, score or date shows straight away.

1. Earn a certificateIn an app’s Progress or Missions page, once every criterion is met.
2. Save its passport recordUse “Passport record (JSON)” next to the certificate download.
3. Drop the records hereThen download your passport file to send, or print the page.

What “verified” means: the record has not been edited since the app made it, and its code matches its scores. The hashes are public and the apps run in the learner’s own browser, so this is a portfolio check, not proof of identity, and not an accreditation.
